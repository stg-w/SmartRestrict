How SmartRestrict Evaluates Food

Database-driven, rule-based classification — transparent, explainable, and scientific.

1. Verified Label Data

Stored nutritional facts per 100g/serving, complete ingredients text, allergens, and dietary classifications from Indian market labels.

2. Dynamic Tag Rules

Configurable statistical thresholds automatically assign concern tags (High Sugar, High Sodium) and positive tags (High Fibre, Low Fat).

3. Restriction Guard

Profiles like Diabetes, High Cholesterol, Low Sodium, and Gluten Intolerance flag conflicting tags and declared allergens with clear reasons.

4. Better Alternatives

Explainable recommendation engine calculates healthier substitutes in the same category, detailing lower sugar, lower sodium, or higher fibre.
